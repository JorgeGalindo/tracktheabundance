Four channels into Europe

How this funding reaches the continent

The public grants database is being retired, so much of this is now advised rather than centralised — but four channels are documented. Click the nodes above to explore each.

① Farm animal welfare
Most active · euro-denominated
The largest confirmed money flow. Coefficient describes itself as the world's largest funder of farm-animal welfare, and Europe is its most active region — €5.5M to Eurogroup for Animals, €2.37M to Spain's Equalia, $3M to Germany's Albert Schweitzer Foundation, plus CiWF, Animal Equality and L214. Mostly multi-year general support.
② AI & biosecurity governance
Policy pipeline
$25.6M to the UK's BlueDot Impact, ~£4M plus a 1:1 matching pledge to the Centre for Long-Term Resilience, and the Brussels Talos fellowship whose fellows are placed at the EU AI Office and OECD.AI. The AI Governance and Technical AI Safety RFPs are open to European applicants.
③ Abundance / progress
Ideas & politics
The newest channel. Collison-backed Progress Ireland saw a housing proposal adopted by the Irish government; Stripe-owned Works in Progress anchors the UK YIMBY node, with a native cluster around it (Centre for British Progress, Looking for Growth, Silicon Continent). The $120M Abundance & Growth Fund remains US-first — a first European grant would signal internationalisation.
④ Effective-giving platforms
Regranting layer
Coefficient's effective-giving RFP funds European donation platforms — Doneer Effectief (Netherlands, >$9M raised in 2025) and Effektiv Spenden (Germany) — which regrant onward: €170k from Effektiv Spenden reached SaferAI in 2025. Money that arrives without appearing in any grants database.
Track the Abundance is an independent research microsite.
